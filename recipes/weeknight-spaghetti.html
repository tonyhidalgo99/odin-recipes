Description

Everyone knows how busy weeknights can be with kids homework, soccer practice, never ending laundry, and on top of all this you still need dinner for a family of 5. I admit I have parental guilt when it comes to my children's diet. Yes, sometimes it really is just Dino nuggets and mac and cheese for dinner. In addition to all of the things that must be done every single day, feeding my kids a nutritious meal that they will actually enjoy and eat is a definite win in my book. This spaghetti recipe that I have been making for several years now is definitely on the weekly rotation. It's simple, relatively quick to make, and best of all, I don't have any guilt feeding this to my picky-eating kids. This can be easily served with a side salad and some sliced up garlic bread, which is what I usually do.

Ingredients

  • 16oz spaghetti (DeCecco is my favorite but any will work)
  • 1 lb ground beef or mild italian sausage
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 3 tsp oregano
  • 3 tsp dry thyme
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 16oz jars any tomato sauce
  • 1/3 block softened neufchatel cream cheese

Steps:

  1. First add about 3 quarts of water to a pot and bring to a boil. While the water is heating up, you can start on the next step, which is the knife work.
  2. Finely dice the onion and mince the garlic cloves. In a pre-heated pan, add some olive oil, the onions, salt and pepper. After about 10 minutes on medium heat, the onions should have some color. Add your ground beef or Italian sausage.
  3. At this point the water should be boiling. Cook the pasta according to package instructions. I usually cook it for 1 minute less than the recommended time.
  4. Cook until the meat has browned. At this point add the minced garlic cloves, oregano and thyme. After about 1 minute, add the tomato paste and stir until everything is mixed.
  5. Open the tomato sauce jar and add everything into the pan with the meat. Add about 1/3 cup of water to the jar, close the lid, and shake in order to remove all of the sauce in the jar. Add this mixture into the same pan. Season with thyme, oregano, salt, and about 2 tsp sugar.
  6. Once the spaghetti has completed its boiling time, add the noodles with tongs directly into the sauce. Be sure to carry over some of the pasta water as well. Remove from the heat, mix everything well, and allow it to rest for about 10 minutes.
